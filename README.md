# Afrimart-kollybright-sales-dashboard
The project analyzes sales data from Afrimart KolllyBright using mircosoft excel, pivot table and a dashboard were created to track revenue perfomance across countries and products.

## Project Overview
The AfriMart KollyBright Sales Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence dashboard designed to analyze sales performance across countries, products, and time periods.

The dashboard transforms sales data into actionable insights by tracking revenue, profit, units sold, and profit margin, while allowing users to explore performance by country, product, and year.

The analysis provides management with a consolidated view of sales performance and helps identify high-performing markets, products, and changes in sales trends over time.

---

## Business Questions Answered
The dashboard was designed to answer questions such as:

- What is the overall revenue and profit generated by the business?
- What is the company's overall profit margin?
- Which countries generate the highest revenue?
- Which countries generate the highest profit?
- Which products contribute the most to profit?
- Which products have the highest unit sales?
- How has revenue changed over time?

## Conclusion
The AfriMart KollyBright Sales Dashboard provides a centralized view of the company's sales and profitability performance across countries, products, and time periods.

With ₦10.45 billion in revenue, ₦2.10 billion in profit, 1.02 million units sold, and a 20.09% profit margin, the dashboard establishes a high-level picture of business performance while allowing users to drill down into the factors contributing to those results.

The analysis can support business decisions around market performance, product profitability, sales trends, inventory planning, and resource allocation.
